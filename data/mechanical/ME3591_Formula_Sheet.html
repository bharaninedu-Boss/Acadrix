Symbols

P = load or power, depending on context

Z = section modulus
Zp = polar section modulus
σ = normal stress; τ = shear stress
σ₁, σ₂ = principal stresses
σm = mean stress; σa = alternating stress
Syt = tensile yield strength; Sut = ultimate tensile strength
S′e = unmodified endurance limit; Se = corrected endurance limit
Ka, Kb, Kc, Kd, Kf = fatigue/notch factors as applicable
W = axial/load force; N = speed in rpm
b, t, l = key/plate dimensions as defined in the problem
f = coefficient of friction; η = efficiency
