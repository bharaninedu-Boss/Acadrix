ME3591 – Design of Machine Elements

ACADRIX Formula Sheet • R-2021

Quick revision reference. Check the applicable design theory, assumptions, standards and PSG Design Data Book before using any formula.

1. Fundamental Design

Factor of safety: FOS = Failure stress / Allowable stress
Normal stress: σ = P/A
Direct shear: τ = P/A
Bending stress: σ = M/Z
Torsional shear: τ = T/Zp
Principal stresses: σ1, σ2 = (σx + σy)/2 ± √[((σx − σy)/2)² + τxy²]
Maximum shear stress: τmax = (σ1 − σ2)/2

2. Shafts and Keys

Power–torque: T = 60P/(2πN) when P is in watts and T in N·m.
Solid shaft torsion: T = (π/16) τ d³
Bending of circular shaft: σb = 32M/(πd³)
Combined bending and torsion: use the specified failure theory and design-code relation.
Key shear: τ = 2T/(d b l)
Key crushing: σc = 4T/(d t l)

3. Couplings and Power Screws

Power screw mean diameter: d2 = (d + dcore)/2
Helix angle: tan α = lead/(πd2)
For trapezoidal threads: μ' = μ sec β, where β is the thread half-angle.
Raising torque: T = Wd2/2 × tan(φ + α)
Lowering torque: T = Wd2/2 × tan(φ − α)

4. Springs, Flywheels and Bearings

Spring stiffness: k = W/δ
Torque from belt: T = (T1 − T2)D/2
Bearing pressure: p = W/(Ld)
Surface speed: v = πdN/60
Bearing friction power: P = fWv

5. Joints and Welds

Fillet-weld throat = 0.707s
Direct weld shear: τ = P/(0.707sL)
Eccentric weld loading: combine direct shear and moment-induced stress using the weld-group properties.
