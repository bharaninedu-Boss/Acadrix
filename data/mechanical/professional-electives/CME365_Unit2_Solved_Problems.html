1. Solar Irradiance on a Surface

Problem: A solar collector receives beam radiation of 700 W/m² and diffuse radiation of 120 W/m² on a horizontal surface. Determine the global radiation.

Given: Gb = 700 W/m², Gd = 120 W/m²

Find: Global radiation, G

Formula: G = Gb + Gd

Substitution: G = 700 + 120

Calculation: G = 820 W/m²

Final answer: 820 W/m².

2. Solar Energy Collected Over an Area

Problem: A 4 m² collector receives an average solar irradiance of 600 W/m² for 5 hours. Determine the incident solar energy.

Given: A = 4 m², G = 600 W/m², t = 5 h

Find: Incident energy

Formula: E = GAt

Substitution: E = 600 × 4 × 5 = 12,000 Wh

Convert: E = 12 kWh

Final answer: 12 kWh of incident solar energy.

3. Useful Heat from a Solar Collector

Problem: A solar thermal collector receives 5 kW of solar power and operates at 60% useful thermal efficiency. Find the useful heat output.

Given: Pin = 5 kW, η = 0.60

Formula: Puseful = ηPin

Substitution: Puseful = 0.60 × 5 = 3 kW

Final answer: 3 kW useful thermal power.

4. Sensible Heat Storage

Problem: A storage tank contains 200 kg of water. Its temperature rises from 30°C to 70°C. Take c = 4.18 kJ/kg·K. Find the sensible heat stored.

Given: m = 200 kg, c = 4.18 kJ/kg·K, ΔT = 70 − 30 = 40 K

Formula: Q = mcΔT

Substitution: Q = 200 × 4.18 × 40 = 33,440 kJ

Convert: Q = 33.44 MJ

Final answer: 33.44 MJ.

5. PV Electrical Power

Problem: A PV module operates at 36 V and delivers 8 A at a particular operating point. Determine its electrical output power.

Given: V = 36 V, I = 8 A

Formula: P = VI

Substitution: P = 36 × 8 = 288 W

Final answer: 288 W.

6. Solar Cell Fill Factor

Problem: A solar cell has VOC = 0.62 V, ISC = 5 A, Vmp = 0.50 V and Imp = 4.2 A. Determine the fill factor.

Given: VOC = 0.62 V, ISC = 5 A, Vmp = 0.50 V, Imp = 4.2 A

Formula: FF = VmpImpVOCISC

Numerator: VmpImp = 0.50 × 4.2 = 2.10 W

Denominator: VOCISC = 0.62 × 5 = 3.10 W

Calculation: FF = 2.10/3.10 = 0.6774

Percentage: FF ≈ 67.7%

Final answer: FF ≈ 0.677 or 67.7%.

7. PV Conversion Efficiency

Problem: A PV module produces 250 W of electrical power while receiving 1000 W/m² irradiance over an area of 2 m². Determine the conversion efficiency.

Given: Pout = 250 W, G = 1000 W/m², A = 2 m²

Input solar power: Pin = GA = 1000 × 2 = 2000 W

Formula: η = PoutPin × 100

Substitution: η = 2502000 × 100 = 12.5%

Final answer: 12.5%.

8. Series Connection of PV Modules

Problem: Four identical PV modules each have a maximum-power voltage of 30 V and maximum-power current of 8 A. If connected in series, determine the array voltage and current at the maximum-power point.

Series rule: Voltages add; current remains the same.

Vmp,array = 4 × 30 = 120 V

Imp,array = 8 A

Final answer: 120 V and 8 A.

9. Parallel Connection of PV Modules

Problem: Three identical PV modules each provide 30 V and 8 A at maximum power. If connected in parallel, determine the array voltage and current.

Parallel rule: Voltage remains the same; currents add.

Vmp,array = 30 V

Imp,array = 3 × 8 = 24 A

Array power: P = 30 × 24 = 720 W

Final answer: 30 V, 24 A and 720 W.

10. Combined PV Array

Problem: Six identical modules each have Vmp = 32 V and Imp = 5 A. Two modules are connected in series per string and three strings are connected in parallel. Find array voltage, current and maximum power.

Series: Varray = 2 × 32 = 64 V

Parallel: Iarray = 3 × 5 = 15 A

Power: Parray = 64 × 15 = 960 W

Final answer: 64 V, 15 A and 960 W.

11. Solar Collector Daily Energy

Problem: A 10 m² collector receives an average useful solar irradiance of 500 W/m² for 6 hours. If the useful thermal efficiency is 55%, find the useful daily energy.

Given: A = 10 m², G = 500 W/m², t = 6 h, η = 0.55

Incident energy: Ein = GAt = 500 × 10 × 6 = 30,000 Wh = 30 kWh

Useful energy: Euseful = ηEin = 0.55 × 30 = 16.5 kWh

Final answer: 16.5 kWh/day.
