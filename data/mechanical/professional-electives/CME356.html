CME356 — Rotating Machinery Design

R-2021 • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Professional Elective

Vertical 53 Credits45 Periods

Advanced design of gas-turbine and other rotating-machine components, including loading, failure criteria, blade/disc design, vibration and damage mechanisms.

Syllabus verified: The official R-2021 Mechanical Engineering curriculum lists CME356 and its five-unit syllabus: Introduction; Designing Forces; Failure Criteria; Blade Design; Blade Vibrations and Damage Mechanisms.

Unit-wise Notes

Unit I — Introduction

Gas-turbine operating regimes; creep and fatigue damage; material, design and operational parameters; experimental characterisation.

📘 Open Unit I Notes →

Unit II — Designing Forces

Rotational inertia, flight, shaft precession, pressure gradient, torsion, seizure, blade release, mountings, bearings and major component loads.

📘 Open Unit II Notes →

Unit III — Failure Criteria

Proof/ultimate strength, failure theories, creep, fatigue, S-N/R-M diagrams, stress concentration, mean stress, cumulative fatigue, Goodman diagram and Larson-Miller parameter.

📘 Open Unit III Notes →

Unit IV — Blade Design

Disc and blade stresses, axial-flow blade design, blade leaning, flanges, bolted structures, leakage and fatigue failure.

📘 Open Unit IV Notes →

Unit V — Blade Vibrations and Damage Mechanisms

Natural frequencies, blade twist, centrifugal stiffening, excitation, rotating stall, flutter, Campbell diagram, resonance and temperature effects.
