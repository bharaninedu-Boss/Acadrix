Professional Electives

The ACADRIX Professional Electives catalogue organizes the R-2021 Mechanical Engineering elective verticals, semester slots and currently available study resources in one place.

How the vertical system works: Professional Electives are registered in Semesters V and VI. Students may choose courses from one or different verticals, but only one course is selected from a row in a semester. Two courses from the same row are permitted when one is taken in Semester V and the other in Semester VI. source
