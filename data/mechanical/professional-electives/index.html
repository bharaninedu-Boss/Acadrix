Professional Electives

A dedicated ACADRIX catalogue for the R-2021 Mechanical Engineering Professional Elective verticals, semester slots and available study resources.

How the vertical system works: Professional Electives are registered in Semesters V and VI. Students may choose courses from one or different verticals, but only one course is selected from a row in a semester. Two courses from the same row are permitted when one is taken in Semester V and the other in Semester VI.
