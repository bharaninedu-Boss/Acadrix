CME358 — Industrial Layout Design and Safety

R-2021 • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Professional Elective

Vertical 52-0-2-330 Theory + 30 Lab Periods

Industrial facility layout, layout algorithms, material handling, warehousing, safety planning and hazard-control practices.

Syllabus verified: CME358 is listed under Process Equipment and Piping Design. The course combines theory with an Industrial Layout Design Laboratory.

Unit-wise Notes

Unit I — Introduction

Industrial facility layout, layout problems, product analysis, equipment selection, personnel and space requirements, process/material flow and layout data.

📘 Open Unit I Notes →

Unit II — Facilities Layout Design & Algorithms

Traditional approaches, Systematic Layout Planning, office-layout considerations, code compliance, OSHA/ADA, construction/improvement/hybrid algorithms and layout software.

Notes coming next

Unit III — Layout Problem Models & Algorithms

Single-row and multi-row models, equal/unequal department areas, material handling, storage and warehousing design.

Notes coming next

Unit IV — Safety Planning & Management

Safety programming, procedures, performance measures, education/training, accident overview, occupational health, industrial hygiene, hazardous substances and Factories Act 1948.

Notes coming next

Unit V — Approaches in Safety Management

Trips/slips/falls, electrocution, static electricity, hazardous energy, forklift and tractor hazards, and safe storage/handling of cylinders, corrosives, hydrocarbons and waste containers.
