1. Biomass Energy from a Given Mass

Problem: A biomass sample has a calorific value of 16 MJ/kg. If 250 kg is available, find its theoretical energy content.

Formula: E = m × CV.

E = 250 × 16 = 4000 MJ.

Since 1 GJ = 1000 MJ, E = 4 GJ.

Final answer: Theoretical energy content = 4000 MJ = 4 GJ.

2. Useful Heat from Biomass Combustion

Problem: 100 kg biomass has calorific value 18 MJ/kg. If combustion-system efficiency is 70%, find useful heat.

Total energy = 100 × 18 = 1800 MJ.

Useful heat = 0.70 × 1800 = 1260 MJ.

Final answer: Useful heat = 1260 MJ.

3. Biogas Energy

Problem: A biogas plant produces 80 m³ of biogas per day. Take calorific value as 21 MJ/m³. Find daily energy content.

E = 80 × 21 = 1680 MJ/day.

Final answer: Biogas energy = 1680 MJ/day.

4. Useful Energy from Biogas

Problem: The biogas production is 60 m³/day, calorific value 21 MJ/m³, and conversion efficiency 35%. Find useful daily energy.

Total energy = 60 × 21 = 1260 MJ/day.

Useful energy = 0.35 × 1260 = 441 MJ/day.

Final answer: Useful energy = 441 MJ/day.

5. Gasifier Conversion Efficiency

Problem: Biomass input contains 5000 MJ of energy. A gasification system converts 65% of the input energy into producer-gas energy. Find output energy.

Egas = 0.65 × 5000 = 3250 MJ.

Final answer: Producer-gas energy = 3250 MJ.

6. Biodiesel Yield

Problem: An oil-processing unit receives 500 kg of feedstock containing 80% recoverable oil. If transesterification gives 90% biodiesel yield from recovered oil, find biodiesel produced.

Recovered oil = 500 × 0.80 = 400 kg.

Biodiesel = 400 × 0.90 = 360 kg.

Final answer: Biodiesel production = 360 kg.

7. Biogas Plant Retention-Time Check

Problem: A digester receives 12 m³/day of slurry. If hydraulic retention time is 30 days, estimate required digester volume.

Vdigester = Q × HRT

V = 12 × 30 = 360 m³.

Final answer: Required digester volume = 360 m³.

8. Cogeneration Useful Output

Problem: A biomass CHP system receives 1000 MJ fuel energy. Electrical efficiency is 25% and useful heat recovery is 45%. Find electrical energy and useful heat.

Electrical output = 0.25 × 1000 = 250 MJ.

Useful heat = 0.45 × 1000 = 450 MJ.

Total useful energy = 250 + 450 = 700 MJ.

Final answer: Electrical = 250 MJ; useful heat = 450 MJ; total useful output = 700 MJ.
