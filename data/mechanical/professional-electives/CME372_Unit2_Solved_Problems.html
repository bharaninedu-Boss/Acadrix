Problem 1 — Quadratic Bezier Curve at a Parameter

Given: Control points P₀=(0,0), P₁=(4,6), P₂=(8,0), u=0.5.
Find: point on the curve.
Formula: P(u) = (1−u)²P₀ + 2(1−u)uP₁ + u²P₂.
Substitute: P(0.5) = (0.5)²(0,0) + 2(0.5)(0.5)(4,6) + (0.5)²(8,0).
Arithmetic: First contribution = (0,0); second = (2,3); third = (2,0).
Final: P(0.5) = (4,3).

Problem 2 — Cubic Bezier Curve

Given: P₀=(0,0), P₁=(2,4), P₂=(6,4), P₃=(8,0), u=0.5.
Formula: P(u)=(1−u)³P₀+3(1−u)²uP₁+3(1−u)u²P₂+u³P₃.
Substitute: coefficients at u=0.5 are 0.125, 0.375, 0.375, 0.125.
X: 0.125(0)+0.375(2)+0.375(6)+0.125(8)=4.
Y: 0.125(0)+0.375(4)+0.375(4)+0.125(0)=3.
Final: P(0.5)=(4,3).

Problem 3 — Cubic Bezier Endpoint Tangent

Given: Cubic Bezier control points P₀=(1,2), P₁=(4,5), P₂=(7,4), P₃=(9,1).
Find: tangent vector at the start.
Formula: P′(0)=3(P₁−P₀).
Substitute: P′(0)=3[(4−1),(5−2)] = 3(3,3).
Final: P′(0)=(9,9).

Problem 4 — Linear Interpolation for a Parametric Surface

Given: A surface patch has boundary position values along a parameter direction of 20 mm at u=0 and 50 mm at u=1. Estimate the interpolated value at u=0.4 for a linear variation.
Formula: q(u)=(1−u)q₀+uq₁.
Substitute: q(0.4)=0.6(20)+0.4(50).
Arithmetic: 12+20=32 mm.
Final: q=32 mm. This is a simple interpolation practice, not a complete Coons-surface calculation.

Problem 5 — CSG Volume

Given: A block is 100×60×40 mm. A cylindrical through-hole of diameter 20 mm passes through the full 40 mm thickness.
Find: remaining volume using the CSG difference concept.
Step 1 — Block volume: Vb=100×60×40=240,000 mm³.
Step 2 — Hole radius: r=20/2=10 mm.
Step 3 — Cylinder volume: Vc=πr²h=π(10)²(40)=4,000π ≈ 12,566.37 mm³.
Step 4 — Difference: V=240,000−12,566.37=227,433.63 mm³.
Final: Remaining volume ≈ 227,433.63 mm³.

Problem 6 — B-Rep Entity Identification

Question: A rectangular solid has 8 vertices, 12 edges and 6 faces. Identify its basic B-Rep boundary entities.
Answer: Vertices define points, edges connect vertices and faces bound the solid. Thus the B-Rep contains 8 vertices + 12 edges + 6 faces, together with the topology connecting them.
