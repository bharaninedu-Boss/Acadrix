MECHANICAL ENGINEERING · R-2021 · VERTICAL 2

CME338 — Value Engineering

A 3-credit Professional Elective covering value engineering concepts, the job plan, analytical techniques, worksheets and applications.

PEC3-0-03 Credits45 Periods
Official syllabus basis: This page follows the Anna University R-2021 Mechanical Engineering syllabus for CME338. The official syllabus lists five units of 9 periods each and the prescribed textbooks by Iyer and Anil Kumar & Mukhopadhyaya.

Unit-wise study plan

Unit I — Value Engineering Basics

Origin, definitions, value analysis, functions, cost, worth, creativity, uses and limitations.

Unit II — Job Plan & Process

Seven phases, FAST diagramming, behavioural and organisational aspects, principles and benefits.

Unit III — Value Engineering Techniques

Creativity, brainstorming, Gordon technique, morphological analysis, decision-making, FCWA, FAST, break-even and LCC.

Unit IV — Worksheets & Guidelines

Worksheets, function classification, cost analysis, idea comparison, feasibility, proposals, financial aspects, LCC, presentation and audit.

Unit V — Versatility of Value Engineering

Maintenance and repair, non-hardware projects, programme initiation, training and career development.
