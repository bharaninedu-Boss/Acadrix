Problem 1 — Two-Node Bar Stiffness

Given: E = 200 GPa, A = 500 mm², L = 1,000 mm.
Find: axial stiffness coefficient EA/L.
Convert: E = 200,000 N/mm².
Formula: k = EA/L.
Substitute: k = (200,000 × 500)/1,000.
Arithmetic: 100,000,000/1,000 = 100,000 N/mm.
Final: k = 100,000 N/mm.

Problem 2 — Bar Elongation from Nodal Displacement

Given: A two-node bar has E = 200,000 N/mm² and A = 300 mm². Nodal displacements are u₁ = 0 mm and u₂ = 0.20 mm. Length L = 500 mm.
Find: average axial strain and stress.
Step 1 — Strain: ε = (u₂−u₁)/L = (0.20−0)/500 = 0.0004.
Step 2 — Stress: σ = Eε = 200,000 × 0.0004 = 80 N/mm².
Final: ε = 4.0×10⁻⁴; σ = 80 MPa.

Problem 3 — Nodal Force for the Same Bar

Given: From Problem 2, k = EA/L = 120,000 N/mm and u₂−u₁ = 0.20 mm.
Find: axial force magnitude.
Formula: F = kΔu.
Substitute: F = 120,000 × 0.20.
Arithmetic: F = 24,000 N.
Final: axial force magnitude = 24 kN.

Problem 4 — Two Equal Bar Elements in Series

Given: Two identical axial bar elements are connected in series. Each has E = 200,000 N/mm², A = 100 mm² and L = 500 mm. A tensile load of 10 kN is applied at the free end. Neglect self-weight.
Find: total elongation.
Step 1 — Element stiffness: k = EA/L = (200,000×100)/500 = 40,000 N/mm.
Step 2 — Each element force: For series elements carrying the same axial load, F = 10,000 N.
Step 3 — Each elongation: δ = FL/EA = (10,000×500)/(200,000×100) = 0.25 mm.
Step 4 — Total: δtotal = 0.25 + 0.25 = 0.50 mm.
Final: total elongation = 0.50 mm.

Problem 5 — Thermal FEM Concept

Question: A one-dimensional wall is divided into finite elements for steady heat conduction. Temperature is prescribed at one boundary and convection is specified at the other. What is the FEM solution sequence?
Answer: Define wall geometry and conductivity → mesh → select thermal element → form element thermal equations → assemble global thermal system → apply prescribed-temperature and convection boundary conditions → solve nodal temperatures → calculate heat flux.
Final: Temperature is the principal nodal unknown in the basic thermal FEM formulation.

Problem 6 — Shape-Function Interpolation

Given: In a simple one-dimensional element, nodal values are u₁ = 2 mm and u₂ = 6 mm. At a location, N₁ = 0.25 and N₂ = 0.75.
Find: interpolated u.
Formula: u = N₁u₁ + N₂u₂.
Substitute: u = 0.25(2) + 0.75(6).
Arithmetic: u = 0.50 + 4.50 = 5.00 mm.
Final: interpolated displacement = 5.00 mm.
