CME380 — Exam Preparation

Automobile Engineering • R-2021 • Anna University

A focused revision pack for Part A 2-mark questions and Part B 13-mark answers. Use the unit notes first, then this page for final revision.

Verified syllabus basis: CME380 has five units: Vehicle Structure and Engines; Engine Auxiliary Systems; Transmission Systems; Steering, Brakes and Suspension Systems; and Alternative Energy Sources. The official syllabus lists each unit for 9 periods. Official syllabus.

Verified 2024 Part A pattern

The April/May 2024 CME380 paper (Question Paper Code 50771) is a verified R-2021 university paper. Its ten 2-mark questions covered chassis systems, engine components, unit injection, three-way catalytic converter, clutch comparison, slip/universal joints, steering gear, ABS, natural gas/LPG constituents, and electric versus hybrid vehicles.

What this tells us: every unit can appear in Part A, and short questions commonly test definitions, functions and differences.

Important 13-mark question bank

  1. Explain vehicle construction, chassis/frame/body and major vehicle layouts with neat diagrams.
  2. Explain vehicle aerodynamic resistances and moments and their effect on vehicle performance.
  3. Explain VVT with construction/working and advantages.
  4. Explain electronically controlled diesel injection systems, comparing unit injector, rotary distributor and CRDI.
  5. Explain electronic ignition systems and compare transistorized coil ignition with capacitive-discharge ignition.
  6. Explain WGT and VGT turbochargers with construction, working and comparison.
  7. Explain the three-way catalytic converter and its role in emission control.
  8. Explain clutch construction and compare single-plate and multi-plate clutches.
  9. Explain manual/automatic transmission systems and gear-shift mechanisms.
  10. Explain torque converter or fluid flywheel with neat diagram.
  11. Explain differential and rear axle arrangement with power flow.
  12. Explain steering geometry and power steering.
  13. Explain hydraulic and pneumatic braking systems and compare them.
  14. Explain ABS, EBD and traction control with operating sequence.
  15. Explain alternative fuels used in automobiles and the required engine modifications.
  16. Compare SI/CI engine performance, combustion and emission characteristics when using alternate fuels.
  17. Explain electric and hybrid vehicles with neat block diagrams and compare them.
  18. Explain the working of a fuel-cell vehicle and the role of the fuel cell.

Part A — 2-mark revision list

  • Functions of chassis, compression ring, connecting rod.
  • Unit injector versus conventional diesel injection.
  • Three-way catalytic converter and engine in which it is used.
  • Single-plate versus multi-plate clutch.
  • Functions of slip and universal joints.
  • Function and types of steering gear.
  • ABS and conditions for effective operation.
  • Major constituents of natural gas and LPG.
  • Electric vehicle versus hybrid vehicle.
  • Functions of VVT, torque converter, differential, master cylinder, EBD and traction control.

Last-day revision order

  1. First: revise all diagrams — CRDI, turbocharger, catalytic converter, clutch, torque converter, differential, steering, brake/ABS, EV/hybrid and fuel cell.
  2. Second: memorize definitions, functions and comparisons for Part A.
  3. Third: prepare at least 3 long answers from each unit.
  4. Fourth: revise advantages, limitations, applications and comparison tables.
  5. Finally: practise writing one complete 13-mark answer in 12–15 minutes.

Best answer structure: Introduction → neat labelled diagram → construction/components → working → important features/parameters → advantages & limitations → applications → short conclusion.
