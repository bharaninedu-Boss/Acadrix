Problem 1 — Per-Capita Energy Consumption

Given: Annual energy consumption = 1,500 PJ; population = 50 million.
Find: average annual per-capita energy consumption.
Formula: Per-capita consumption = Total energy consumption / Population.
Substitute: = 1,500 PJ / 50 million persons.
Convert: 1,500 PJ = 1,500 × 1015 J; 50 million = 50 × 106 persons.
Arithmetic: = (1,500 × 1015) / (50 × 106) = 30 × 109 J/person.
Final answer: 30 GJ/person·year.

Problem 2 — Capacity Factor

Given: Wind turbine rated power = 2 MW; annual energy generated = 3,504 MWh. Assume 8,760 h/year.
Find: capacity factor.
Formula: Capacity factor = Actual energy generated / (Rated power × available time).
Substitute: = 3,504 / (2 × 8,760).
Arithmetic: Denominator = 17,520 MWh; capacity factor = 3,504 / 17,520 = 0.20.
Final answer: Capacity factor = 0.20 = 20%.

Problem 3 — Sector-wise Energy Share

Given: Total energy consumption = 1,000 units. Domestic = 200, industrial = 450, commercial = 150, agriculture = 100 and transportation = 100 units.
Find: industrial-sector share.
Formula: Sector share = Sector consumption / Total consumption × 100.
Substitute: = 450 / 1,000 × 100.
Final answer: Industrial share = 45%.

Problem 4 — Future Demand Growth

Given: Present annual energy demand = 800 GWh. Assume demand grows at 5% per year for 2 years.
Find: projected demand.
Formula: Future demand = Present demand × (1 + growth rate)n.
Substitute: = 800 × (1 + 0.05)2.
Arithmetic: = 800 × 1.1025 = 882 GWh.
Final answer: Projected demand = 882 GWh.

Problem 5 — Renewable Contribution

Given: Total annual electricity generation = 5,000 GWh; renewable generation = 1,250 GWh.
Find: renewable contribution.
Formula: Renewable contribution = Renewable generation / Total generation × 100.
Substitute: = 1,250 / 5,000 × 100.
Final answer: Renewable contribution = 25%.
