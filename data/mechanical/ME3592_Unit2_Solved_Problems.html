ME3592 Unit II — Solved Problems

Problem 1 — Sine Bar Angle

Given: Distance between roller centres, L = 200 mm. Slip-gauge height, h = 50 mm.

Find: Angle set by the sine bar.

Step 1: Principle

sin θ = h / L

Step 2: Substitute

sin θ = 50 / 200 = 0.25

Step 3: Calculate

θ = sin⁻¹(0.25) = 14.48°

Answer: θ ≈ 14°29′.

Exam method: draw the sine-bar arrangement, state the principle, write sin θ = h/L, substitute and convert the final decimal angle if required.

Problem 2 — Mean and Range of Repeated Measurements

Given: Five readings of a dimension are 20.02, 20.04, 20.03, 20.01 and 20.05 mm.

Find: Mean and range.

Step 1: Mean

Mean = (20.02 + 20.04 + 20.03 + 20.01 + 20.05) / 5

Mean = 100.15 / 5 = 20.03 mm

Step 2: Range

Range = maximum reading − minimum reading

Range = 20.05 − 20.01 = 0.04 mm

Answer: Mean = 20.03 mm; range = 0.04 mm.

Problem 3 — Comparator Deviation

Given: A comparator is zeroed using a 25.000 mm standard. The workpiece produces a positive deviation of +0.012 mm.

Find: Workpiece dimension.

Step 1: Understand the comparator

The comparator reading represents deviation from the preset standard.

Step 2: Add the deviation

Workpiece size = standard size + deviation

= 25.000 + 0.012 = 25.012 mm

Answer: 25.012 mm.

Common mistake: do not treat a comparator as an absolute measuring instrument unless the instrument/setup is specifically designed and calibrated for that purpose.
