Example 1 • ACADRIX Practice

Direct + Bending Stress

Problem: A rectangular section has width 40 mm and thickness 20 mm. It carries an axial tensile load of 20 kN and a bending moment of 1.2 kN·m about the axis associated with the 40 mm width. Determine the direct stress, bending stress and maximum tensile stress.

Step 1 — Given
P = 20 kN = 20,000 N
M = 1.2 kN·m = 1.2 × 106 N·mm
b = 40 mm, h = 20 mm
Step 2 — Area
A = bh = 40 × 20 = 800 mm²
Step 3 — Direct stress
σd = P/A = 20,000/800 = 25 N/mm²
Step 4 — Section modulus
Z = bh²/6 = (40 × 20²)/6 = 2666.67 mm³
Step 5 — Bending stress
σb = M/Z = (1.2 × 106)/2666.67 = 450 N/mm²
Step 6 — Maximum tensile stress
σmax = σd + σb = 25 + 450 = 475 N/mm²

Answer: Direct stress = 25 MPa; bending stress = 450 MPa; maximum tensile stress = 475 MPa.

Example 2 • ACADRIX Practice

Eccentric Loading

Problem: A component carries a load P = 12 kN with an eccentricity e = 50 mm. If the resisting section has area A = 1000 mm² and section modulus Z = 5000 mm³, determine the direct stress, eccentric moment and extreme bending stress.

Step 1 — Direct stress
σd = P/A = 12,000/1000 = 12 MPa
Step 2 — Eccentric moment
M = P × e = 12,000 × 50 = 600,000 N·mm
Step 3 — Bending stress
σb = M/Z = 600,000/5000 = 120 MPa
Step 4 — Extreme stresses
σmax = σd + σb = 132 MPa
σmin = σd − σb = −108 MPa

Answer: Direct stress = 12 MPa; moment = 600 N·m; extreme stresses = 132 MPa and −108 MPa.

Example 3 • ACADRIX Practice

Principal Stresses under Plane Stress

Problem: At a point in a machine component, σx = 80 MPa, σy = 20 MPa and τxy = 30 MPa. Determine the principal stresses and maximum shear stress.

σ1,2 = (σx + σy)/2 ± √[((σx − σy)/2)² + τxy²]
Step 1
Mean normal stress = (80 + 20)/2 = 50 MPa.
Step 2
Radius = √[(30)² + (30)²] = 42.43 MPa.
Step 3
σ1 = 50 + 42.43 = 92.43 MPa
σ2 = 50 − 42.43 = 7.57 MPa
Step 4 — Maximum shear stress
τmax = (σ1 − σ2)/2 = 42.43 MPa

Answer: σ1 = 92.43 MPa, σ2 = 7.57 MPa, τmax = 42.43 MPa.

Example 4 • ACADRIX Practice

Fluctuating Stress Parameters

Problem: A component experiences σmax = 180 MPa and σmin = −60 MPa. Determine mean stress, alternating stress and stress ratio.

σm = (σmax + σmin)/2 = (180 − 60)/2 = 60 MPa
σa = (σmax − σmin)/2 = (180 + 60)/2 = 120 MPa
R = σmin/σmax = −60/180 = −0.333

Answer: Mean stress = 60 MPa; alternating stress = 120 MPa; stress ratio R = −0.333.

Practice • Solve Yourself

Exam-Style Practice Set

  1. A member carries a direct load and bending moment. Calculate direct, bending and extreme stresses for the given section dimensions.
  2. A load acts at an eccentricity from the centroidal axis. Determine the direct stress, eccentric moment and extreme stresses.
  3. Given σx, σy and τxy, determine the principal stresses and maximum shear stress.
  4. Given maximum and minimum fluctuating stresses, determine mean stress, alternating stress and stress ratio.
  5. For a design problem, apply the failure theory explicitly specified in the question and include the required factor of safety.

12-Mark Answer Workflow

  1. Write Given and Find.
  2. Draw the loading/geometry sketch where required.
  3. Convert all quantities to consistent units.
  4. Write the governing equations before substitution.
  5. Show intermediate calculations clearly.
  6. Apply the specified failure theory; do not silently substitute another theory.
  7. Check strength/stiffness requirements where applicable.
  8. State the final answer with units and practical design selection where required.
