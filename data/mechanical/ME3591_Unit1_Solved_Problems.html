1. Overhang Crank — Diameter by Maximum Shear Stress Theory

Given: P = 1 kN = 1000 N, Syt = 400 N/mm², FOS = 2. Bending arm = 500 mm; torsional arm = 50 + 100 + 25 = 175 mm.

Step 1 — Allowable shear stress
τallow = Syt2 × FOS = 4002 × 2 = 100 N/mm²
Step 2 — Bending moment
M = P × 500 = 1000 × 500 = 500,000 N·mm
Step 3 — Torsional moment
T = P × 175 = 1000 × 175 = 175,000 N·mm
Step 4 — Stresses in solid circular section
σb = 32Mπd³,   τ = 16Tπd³
Step 5 — Maximum shear stress criterion
τmax = √[(σb/2)² + τ²] ≤ τallow
100 = √{[16(500000)πd³]2 + [16(175000)πd³]2}
Step 6 — Solve for diameter
d6 = 256[(500000)² + (175000)²]10000π²
d ≈ 30 mm

Final answer: d ≈ 30 mm.

2. 45C8 Plate — Thickness for Infinite Fatigue Life

Given: Sut = 630 N/mm², completely reversed P = ±50 kN, size factor = 0.85, reliability = 90%, q = 0.8, FOS = 2, D = 100 mm, d = 50 mm, r = 5 mm.

Step 1 — Uncorrected endurance limit
Se′ = 0.5Sut = 0.5(630) = 315 N/mm²
Step 2 — Factors used by the supplied solution

Surface finish factor Ka = 0.76; reliability factor Kc = 0.897. From D/d = 2 and r/d = 0.1, Kt = 2.27.

Kf = 1 + q(Kt − 1) = 1 + 0.8(2.27 − 1) = 2.016
Kd = 1Kf = 0.496
Step 3 — Corrected endurance limit
Se = KaKbKcKdSe′
Se = (0.76)(0.85)(0.897)(0.496)(315) = 90.54 N/mm²
Step 4 — Axial endurance limit and permissible alternating stress
(Se)a = 0.8(90.54) = 72.43 N/mm²
σa = 72.432 = 36.215 N/mm²
Step 5 — Plate thickness
σa = PA = 50,00050t
36.215(50t) = 50,000
1,810.75t = 50,000
t = 50,0001,810.75 = 27.61 mm

Final answer: t ≈ 27.61 mm.

3. Principal Stresses under Combined Loading

Given: σx = 80 MPa, σy = 20 MPa, τxy = 30 MPa.

Step 1 — Mean stress
σavg = 80 + 202 = 50 MPa
Step 2 — Mohr-circle radius
R = √{[(80 − 20)/2]² + 30²} = √1800 = 42.43 MPa
Step 3 — Principal stresses
σ1 = 50 + 42.43 = 92.43 MPa
σ2 = 50 − 42.43 = 7.57 MPa
Step 4 — Maximum shear stress
τmax = 92.43 − 7.572 = 42.43 MPa

Final answer: σ1 = 92.43 MPa, σ2 = 7.57 MPa, τmax = 42.43 MPa.

4. Fluctuating Stress Parameters

Given: σmax = 180 MPa, σmin = −60 MPa.

Step 1 — Mean stress
σm = σmax + σmin2 = 180 − 602 = 60 MPa
Step 2 — Alternating stress
σa = σmax − σmin2 = 180 + 602 = 120 MPa
Step 3 — Stress ratio

5. Eccentric Loading of a Rectangular Section

Given: P = 20 kN = 20,000 N, eccentricity e = 40 mm, area A = 4000 mm², section modulus Z = 80,000 mm³.

Step 1 — Direct stress
σd = PA = 20,0004000 = 5 MPa
Step 2 — Eccentric moment
M = Pe = 20,000 × 40 = 800,000 N·mm
Step 3 — Bending stress
σb = MZ = 800,00080,000 = 10 MPa
Step 4 — Maximum and minimum stresses
σmax = σd + σb = 5 + 10 = 15 MPa
σmin = σd − σb = 5 − 10 = −5 MPa

Final answer: σmax = 15 MPa; σmin = −5 MPa.

6. Solid Shaft under Torsion

Given: T = 1.2 kN·m = 1.2 × 106 N·mm, allowable shear stress τallow = 40 N/mm².

Step 1 — Torsion equation
T = π16τallowd³
Step 2 — Rearrange for diameter
d³ = 16Tπτallow
d³ = 16(1.2 × 106)π(40) ≈ 152,789
d = 3√152,789 ≈ 53.45 mm
Step 3 — Practical selection

Select the next suitable standard shaft diameter from the prescribed design data book and recheck the resulting shear stress.

Calculated minimum diameter: 53.45 mm; final adopted size must be a suitable standard size after checking.
