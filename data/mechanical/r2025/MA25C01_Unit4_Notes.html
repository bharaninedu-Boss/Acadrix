Unit IV — Multiple Integrals

MA25C01 · R-2025 · Exam-oriented notes

Syllabus: iterated integrals/Fubini, double integrals, change of order, Cartesian/polar coordinates, triple integrals and Cartesian/cylindrical/spherical coordinates.

1. Core Notes

Iterated and Double Integrals

For suitable functions and regions, a double integral can be evaluated as successive integrations. For a rectangular region, ∫∫f(x,y)dA becomes an iterated integral in either appropriate order.

Change of Order

When reversing order, first identify or sketch the region, then rewrite the inner and outer limits. Do not merely interchange dx and dy.

Polar Coordinates

x=r cosθ, y=r sinθ, dA=r dr dθ. Use polar coordinates for circular or radial regions.

Triple, Cylindrical and Spherical Integrals

Triple integrals accumulate over volume. Cylindrical coordinates use x=r cosθ, y=r sinθ, z=z with dV=r dr dθ dz. Spherical coordinates use x=ρsinφcosθ, y=ρsinφsinθ, z=ρcosφ with dV=ρ²sinφ dρ dφ dθ.

  • Evaluate a triple integral over a spherical region using spherical coordinates and the correct Jacobian.

5. Quick Revision

Coordinate memory: Cartesian → rectangular geometry; polar → circular planar geometry; cylindrical → circular 3-D geometry; spherical → sphere/radial 3-D geometry. Always write the Jacobian: dA=r dr dθ, dV=r dr dθ dz, dV=ρ²sinφ dρ dφ dθ.
