Unit III — Integral Calculus

MA25C01 · R-2025 · Exam-oriented notes

Syllabus: FTC, indefinite integrals and Net Change Theorem, improper integrals, arc length, area and surface of revolution.

1. Core Notes

Antiderivatives and Integrals

If F′(x)=f(x), then ∫f(x)dx=F(x)+C. For n≠−1, ∫xⁿdx=xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1)+C; ∫1/x dx=ln|x|+C.

Fundamental Theorem and Net Change

If f is continuous, d/dx[∫ₐˣf(t)dt]=f(x) and ∫ₐᵇf(x)dx=F(b)−F(a). Net change equals the integral of the rate: F(b)−F(a)=∫ₐᵇF′(t)dt.

Improper Integrals

An infinite interval or unbounded integrand requires a limit. For example, ∫₁∞1/xᵖdx converges for p>1 and diverges for p≤1.

Area, Arc Length and Surface Area

Area between curves: ∫(upper−lower)dx. Arc length for y=f(x): L=∫√(1+(y′)²)dx. Surface area about the x-axis: S=2π∫|y|√(1+(y′)²)dx.

2. 1-Mark Preparation

  • Define an antiderivative.
  • State the general form of an indefinite integral.
  • State the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
  • What is the Net Change Theorem?
  • Define an improper integral.
  • State the convergence condition for the p-integral from 1 to infinity.
  • Write the formula for arc length of y=f(x).
  • Write the formula for surface area about the x-axis.
  • What does a definite integral represent geometrically?
  • State the power rule for integration.

3. 3-Mark Preparation

  • Explain the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
  • Explain the Net Change Theorem with an engineering example.
  • Distinguish proper and improper integrals.
  • Explain how to set up area between two curves.
  • Derive or explain the arc-length formula.
  • Explain the surface-area formula for revolution.
  • Test a simple improper integral for convergence.

4. 12-Mark Preparation

  • Evaluate a definite/indefinite integral using appropriate integration techniques and show all steps.
  • Evaluate an improper integral using the limiting process and determine convergence.
  • Find the area of a region bounded by curves, including intersection points and correct limits.
  • Find the arc length of a specified curve over an interval.
  • Find the surface area generated by revolving a curve about an axis.

5. Quick Revision

Remember: indefinite integral → +C; definite integral → F(b)−F(a); improper integral → limit; area → upper−lower; arc length → √(1+(y′)²); surface area → 2π(radius)(ds).
