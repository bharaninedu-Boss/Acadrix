Unit II — Functions of Several Variables

MA25C01 · R-2025 · Exam-oriented notes

Syllabus: partial derivatives, chain rule, total derivative, maxima/minima of two variables, Lagrange multipliers and engineering applications.

1. Core Notes

Functions and Partial Derivatives

For z=f(x,y), a partial derivative measures change with respect to one variable while the other is held constant. Higher partials include fₓₓ, fᵧᵧ, fₓᵧ and fᵧₓ.

Chain Rule and Total Derivative

If x and y depend on t, dz/dt=fₓ dx/dt+fᵧ dy/dt. The total differential is dz=fₓdx+fᵧdy.

Maxima and Minima

At an interior stationary point solve fₓ=0 and fᵧ=0. Let D=fₓₓfᵧᵧ−(fₓᵧ)². If D>0 and fₓₓ>0: local minimum; D>0 and fₓₓ<0: local maximum; D<0: saddle point; D=0: inconclusive.

Lagrange Multipliers

For an extremum of f(x,y) subject to g(x,y)=c, solve ∇f=λ∇g together with the constraint.
