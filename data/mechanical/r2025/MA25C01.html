R-2025 Exam Preparation Pattern

1 Mark
Definitions, standard results, terminology, formulas and direct facts.
3 Marks
Short explanations, comparisons, concepts, conditions and compact calculations.
12 Marks
Detailed problems, derivations, applications, multi-step calculations and full solutions.

Important: This 1/3/12-mark organization is an ACADRIX preparation format for R-2025. It does not label any question as an official university question unless separately verified.

Study path: Learn the unit → practise 1-mark recall → master 3-mark concepts → solve 12-mark problems → revise formulas.

Unit-wise Course Structure

Unit I — Differential CalculusFunctions, graphs, limits, continuity, derivatives, maxima/minima, mean value theorem and graph behaviour.

1M → 3M → 12MUnit II — Functions of Several VariablesPartial derivatives, chain rule, total derivative, maxima/minima, Lagrange multipliers and applications.

1M → 3M → 12MUnit III — Integral CalculusFTC, indefinite integrals, net change, improper integrals, arc length, area and surfaces of revolution.

1M → 3M → 12MUnit IV — Multiple IntegralsIterated/double integrals, change of order, Cartesian/polar, triple integrals, cylindrical and spherical coordinates.

1M → 3M → 12M

Course Objectives

  • Model engineering problems using calculus.
  • Solve engineering problems using analytical methods and computational tools.

Course Outcomes

  • CO1: Explain meanings and geometric/physical interpretations of derivatives and integrals.
  • CO2: Apply differentiation and integration to maxima/minima and area-related problems.
  • CO3: Analyze single-variable and multivariable functions.
  • CO4: Use computational software and online platforms to calculate and visualize results.
