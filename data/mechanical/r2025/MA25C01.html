MA25C01 Applied Calculus

Anna University • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Regulation 2025 • Semester 1

ACADRIX study hub for Applied Calculus. The course structure is based on the verified Anna University R-2025 Mechanical Engineering curriculum; ACADRIX notes are independent study material.

R-2025Semester 1BS4 Credits
Study path: Learn the unit → practise problems → verify with questions → revise formulas and key results.

Unit-wise Course Structure

Unit I — Differential CalculusFunctions and graphs, limits, continuity, derivatives, maxima/minima, mean value theorem and graph behaviour. Unit II — Functions of Several VariablesPartial derivatives, chain rule, total derivative, maxima/minima of two variables, Lagrange multipliers and engineering applications. Unit III — Integral CalculusFundamental theorem of calculus, indefinite integrals, net change, improper integrals, arc length, area and surfaces of revolution. Unit IV — Multiple IntegralsIterated and double integrals, change of order, Cartesian/polar coordinates, triple integrals and Cartesian/cylindrical/spherical coordinates.

Course Objectives

  • Model engineering problems using calculus.
  • Solve engineering problems using analytical methods and computational tools.

Course Outcomes

  • CO1: Explain the meanings and geometric/physical interpretations of derivatives and integrals.
  • CO2: Apply differentiation and integration to maxima/minima and area-related problems.
  • CO3: Analyze single-variable and multivariable functions using derivatives and partial derivatives.
  • CO4: Use modern computational software and online platforms to calculate and visualize results.
