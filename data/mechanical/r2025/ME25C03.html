ME25C03 Introduction to Mechanical Engineering

Anna University • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Regulation 2025 • Semester 1

R-2025Semester 1ES (PC) – DIC3 Credits
Course objective: Build fundamental understanding across manufacturing, materials, mechanics and thermal engineering, providing a foundation for later Mechanical Engineering courses.

Unit-wise Course Structure & Notes

Unit I — Engineering

History and evolution of Mechanical Engineering; force, motion, energy, work and power; units and dimensions; SI system; ethics and professionalism.

📘 Open Unit I Notes →
Unit II — Mechanics of Materials and Structures

Stress and strain; tensile, compressive and shear stresses; elasticity and plasticity; strength, toughness and hardness; bending, torsion, axial loading; basic structural analysis and design concepts.

📘 Open Unit II Notes →
Unit III — Energy Interactions

System and energy transfer; conduction, convection and radiation; heat engines; refrigeration and HVAC systems.

📘 Open Unit III Notes →
Unit IV — Machine Elements

Gears, bearings, shafts, fasteners and couplings; selection of machine components; quality control and safety in Mechanical Engineering.

📘 Open Unit IV Notes →
Unit V — Manufacturing Processes

Casting, forming, machining and joining; CNC and additive manufacturing; overview of smart manufacturing.

Activities in the Official Syllabus

  • Interactive quiz and conversion between SI and other unit systems.
  • Demonstration of simple truss or beam problems using software.
  • Working-model demonstrations of an internal combustion engine and refrigerator; virtual thermodynamic-cycle demonstrations.
  • Demonstration of gears, bearings and other machine elements.
  • Machining-process demonstrations and 3D printing of simple parts.
