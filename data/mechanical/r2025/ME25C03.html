ME25C03 Introduction to Mechanical Engineering

Anna University • B.E. Mechanical Engineering • Regulation 2025 • Semester 1

R-2025Semester 1ES (PC) – DIC3 Credits
Course objective: Build fundamental understanding across manufacturing, materials, mechanics and thermal engineering, providing a foundation for later Mechanical Engineering courses.

Unit-wise Course Structure

Unit I — EngineeringHistory and evolution of Mechanical Engineering; force, motion, energy, work and power; units and dimensions; SI system; ethics and professionalism.
Unit II — Mechanics of Materials and StructuresStress and strain; tensile, compressive and shear stresses; elasticity and plasticity; strength, toughness and hardness; bending, torsion, axial loading; basic structural analysis and design concepts.
Unit III — Energy InteractionsSystem and energy transfer; conduction, convection and radiation; heat engines; refrigeration and HVAC systems.
Unit IV — Machine ElementsGears, bearings, shafts, fasteners and couplings; selection of machine components; quality control and safety in Mechanical Engineering.
Unit V — Manufacturing ProcessesCasting, forming, machining and joining; CNC and additive manufacturing; overview of smart manufacturing.

Activities in the Official Syllabus

  • Interactive quiz and conversion between SI and other unit systems.
  • Demonstration of simple truss or beam problems using software.
  • Working-model demonstrations of an internal combustion engine and refrigerator; virtual thermodynamic-cycle demonstrations.
  • Demonstration of gears, bearings and other machine elements.
  • Machining-process demonstrations and 3D printing of simple parts.
