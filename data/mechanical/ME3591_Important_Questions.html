ME3591 — Important Questions

Design of Machine Elements • R-2021 • Semester 5

How this list is built: PYQ-based questions are taken from the verified April/May 2024 ME3591 paper currently available in ACADRIX. Syllabus focus areas are included separately. This is a revision guide, not a prediction of the next paper.

Part A — 2-Mark Questions

Q1
Distinguish between design synthesis and design analysis.
Q2
Name two machine components that may fail due to excessive elastic deflection.
Q3
State the disadvantages of a hollow shaft compared with a solid shaft.
Q4
What is the function of a coupling?
Q5

Q6

Q7

Q8

Long-Answer / Numerical Practice

Unit I
Design the diameter of an overhang crank subjected to bending and torsional loading using the specified failure theory and factor of safety.
Unit I
Determine the thickness of a plate subjected to completely reversed axial loading for infinite fatigue life, considering surface, size, reliability and notch effects.
Unit II
Design a square key for a shaft transmitting specified power at a given speed. Determine suitable key dimensions from strength requirements.
Unit III
Design a muff coupling for two shafts transmitting power. Determine shaft, sleeve and key dimensions using allowable stresses.
Unit III
For a double-threaded ISO metric trapezoidal power screw, calculate raising torque, lowering torque and efficiency including thread friction.
Unit III
Determine the size of a fillet weld for a rectangular welded joint subjected to an eccentric load and bending moment.
Unit IV
For a 360° hydrodynamic journal bearing, determine coefficient of friction, power loss, minimum film thickness, oil flow and temperature rise using the appropriate bearing charts/data.
Unit V
Design a line shaft carrying pulleys and subjected to belt tensions. Determine reactions, bending moments and shaft diameter using ASME shaft design criteria.
Unit V
Determine the dimensions of a cotter/socket joint for given rod and cotter sizes using tensile, compressive and shear allowable stresses.

Syllabus Focus Areas

Unit I: failure theories, combined loading, principal stresses, eccentric loading, stress concentration, fluctuating stresses and endurance limit.
Unit II: shaft design, keys and related strength checks.
Unit III: couplings, power screws and welded joints.
Unit IV: springs, flywheels and hydrodynamic bearings.
Unit V: cotter/knuckle joints and machine-component design applications.
