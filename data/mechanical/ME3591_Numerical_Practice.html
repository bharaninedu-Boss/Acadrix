Exam Workflow

  1. Write the given data with units.
  2. Draw or identify the relevant loading and geometry.
  3. Write the governing formula before substitution.
  4. Substitute every supplied numerical value explicitly.
  5. Show intermediate arithmetic and units.
  6. Check dimensions, stress limits, rigidity or strength.
  7. Select a practical standard dimension only after the theoretical calculation.
  8. State the final answer with units and, where relevant, the adopted standard size.

Evidence rule: ✓ VERIFIED PYQ is used only when the source paper is explicitly identified. ✦ ACADRIX PRACTICE means a study problem created for preparation and is not an official university PYQ.
