Exam Workflow

  1. Write the given data with units.
  2. Draw or identify the relevant loading/geometry.
  3. Write the governing formula before substitution.
  4. Show intermediate calculations.
  5. Check dimensions, stress limits and units.
  6. Round the final design dimension to a practical standard value when the problem requires selection.
