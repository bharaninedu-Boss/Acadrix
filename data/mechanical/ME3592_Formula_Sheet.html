ME3592 — Formula Sheet

R-2021 • Metrology and Measurements • Semester V

Quick numerical reference arranged unit-wise. Use the formula together with the exact measurement convention, standard and data given in the question.

⚠️ Exam rule: For a numerical answer write Given → Formula → Substitution → Calculation → Unit → Final answer/interpretation. Do not invent IS 919, gauge-tolerance or instrument constants when the question does not supply them.

Unit I — Measurement, Errors & Uncertainty

Mean

x̄ = Σxᵢn

Sample standard deviation

s = √[ Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n − 1) ]

Type A uncertainty of mean

uA = s√n

Rectangular Type B

uB = a√3

Combined standard uncertainty

uc = √(u1² + u2² + …)

Expanded uncertainty

U = k uc

For independent components with sensitivity coefficients, the general uncertainty propagation uses the appropriate coefficient-weighted contributions. State assumptions clearly.

Unit II — Linear, Angular, Thread & Gear Measurement

Vernier caliper

Reading = Main scale reading + (Vernier coincidence × least count)

Sine bar

sin θ = hL  ⇒  θ = sin⁻¹(h/L)

Autocollimator

Reflected angular deviation ≈ 2θ  ⇒  θ ≈ deviation/2

Three-wire thread measurement

Use the specified three-wire measurement relation and the given thread geometry; substitute the question's constants rather than memorising an unstated convention.

For micrometer, height gauge, depth micrometer, bore gauge and comparators, use the instrument's least-count/setting relation supplied or established by the question.

Unit III — Tolerancing & Process Capability

Tolerance

Tolerance = Upper limit − Lower limit

Clearance fit

Maximum clearance = Maximum hole − Minimum shaft
Minimum clearance = Minimum hole − Maximum shaft

Interference

Maximum interference = Maximum shaft − Minimum hole
Minimum interference = Minimum shaft − Maximum hole

Worst-case stack-up

Tstack = T₁ + T₂ + … + Tₙ

RSS stack-up

TRSS = √(T₁² + T₂² + … + Tₙ²)

Process capability

Cp = USL − LSL6σ
Cpk = min[ USL − μ3σ, μ − LSL3σ ]

For IS 919 limits-and-fits problems, obtain the required deviations/fundamental tolerance values from the specified table; do not fabricate table values.

Unit IV — Surface Metrology

Observed deviation variation

Variation = Maximum measured deviation − Minimum measured deviation

Ra

Ra = 1L ∫₀ᴸ |y(x)| dx

Sa

Sa = 1A ∬A |z(x,y)| dA

Ra is a profile parameter; Sa is an areal/3D parameter. Surface roughness results depend on the applicable measurement/evaluation conditions.

Unit V — Advances in Metrology

Laser interferometry

Use the displacement/path-difference relation specified by the interferometer arrangement; identify whether the optical path changes once or twice before substituting.

Straightness/alignment

Angular error × measurement length gives the corresponding small-angle displacement contribution when the stated geometry permits the approximation.

CMM

Coordinate distance: d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² + (z₂−z₁)²]

Machine vision, CT and white-light scanning are primarily procedure/working topics in this syllabus; use the equations actually supplied by a particular numerical question.

Last-Minute Numerical Checklist

  1. Write every given value with units.
  2. Choose the formula matching the measurement method.
  3. Keep dimensions consistent.
  4. Show every substitution step.
  5. Do not round too early.
  6. State the final value with units.
  7. For acceptance questions, compare with the specified tolerance/limit and state pass or fail only when the criterion is defined.
