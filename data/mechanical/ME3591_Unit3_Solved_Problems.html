1. Rectangular Fillet-Weld Group — Practice

Generated practice: This example is an exam-style numerical for weld-group practice; it is not an official PYQ.

Given: P = 25 kN, e = 500 mm, rectangular weld-group dimensions = 150 mm × 100 mm, permissible weld shear stress = 75 N/mm².

To find: Fillet-weld size s.

Step 1 — Effective throat and weld area

t = 0.707s
L = 2(150 + 100) = 500 mm
Aw = 0.707s × 500 = 353.5s mm²

Step 2 — Direct shear

τd = PAw
τd = 25,000/(353.5s) = 70.72/s N/mm²

Step 3 — Eccentric moment

M = P × e
M = 25,000 × 500 = 12.5 × 10⁶ N·mm

Step 4 — Combined weld stress

For the stated practice geometry, σb = 785.8/s N/mm².
τmax = 12√(σb² + 4τd²)
75 = 1/2√[(785.8/s)² + 4(70.72/s)²]
22,500s² = 637,488.1
s = 5.32 mm

Step 5 — Adopt and check

Adopt s = 6 mm.
τd = 11.79 N/mm²
σb = 130.97 N/mm²
τmax ≈ 66.56 N/mm²

66.56 < 75 N/mm² → Safe.

Final answer: Adopt 6 mm fillet weld.

2. Cotter Joint — Practice

Generated practice: Exam-style cotter-joint calculation; not an official PYQ.

Given: Rod diameter d = 50 mm; 40C8 steel; Syt = 380 N/mm²; FOS = 6; Syc = 2Syt; Ssy = 0.5Syt.

To find: Axial design load and initial socket dimensions.

Step 1 — Allowable stresses

σt,allow = 380/6 = 63.33 N/mm²
σc,allow = (2 × 380)/6 = 126.67 N/mm²
τallow = (0.5 × 380)/6 = 31.67 N/mm²

Step 2 — Axial load corresponding to the rod section

P = πd²4σt,allow
P = (π/4)(50)²(63.33)
P ≈ 124,348 N

Step 3 — Component checks

Check the rod, spigot, socket and cotter for tensile, shear, bearing and crushing failure as applicable. Calculate each theoretical dimension from its governing equation.

Use the permitted PSG Design Data Book for the final standard proportions and dimensions.

Exam conclusion: Do not stop after sizing the rod; the cotter and connected sections must also be checked.

3. Eccentrically Loaded Bolted Joint — Practice Method

Generated practice method: Use this workflow when numerical values are supplied in an examination question.

Given: Applied load P, eccentricity e, number and positions of bolts, and allowable bolt stress.

Step 1 — Locate bolt-group centroid

Mark the centroid of the bolt arrangement and measure each bolt's radial distance from it.

Step 2 — Direct load

Fd = Pn

Step 3 — Eccentric moment

M = P × e

Step 4 — Moment-induced load

Determine the additional load on each bolt according to its distance from the centroid and the bolt-group procedure specified by the prescribed design method.

Step 5 — Critical bolt

Combine the direct and moment-induced effects at the most heavily loaded bolt and compare with the allowable condition.

Final: State the critical bolt and whether the design is safe.

4. Weld Group under Eccentric Load — Exam Workflow

Generated practice workflow: Numerical structure only; do not treat it as a PYQ.

  1. Draw the weld group and load.
  2. Convert weld size to effective throat.
  3. Calculate weld-group area.
  4. Calculate direct stress/shear.
  5. Calculate M = P × e.
  6. Determine the moment-induced stress distribution using the weld-group geometry.
  7. Find the critical weld location.
  8. Combine stresses using the specified design criterion.
  9. Calculate theoretical weld size.
  10. Adopt a practical size and re-check.
