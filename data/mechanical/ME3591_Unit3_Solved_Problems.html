1. Rectangular Fillet-Weld Group — Practice

Generated practice: Exam-style weld-group numerical; not an official PYQ.

Given: P = 25 kN = 25,000 N; eccentricity e = 500 mm; rectangular weld group = 150 mm × 100 mm; permissible weld shear stress = 75 N/mm².

To find: Fillet-weld size s.

Step 1 — Effective throat and weld length

t = 0.707s
L = 2(150 + 100) = 500 mm
Aw = tL = 0.707s(500) = 353.5s mm²

Step 2 — Direct shear

τd = PAw
τd = 25,000/353.5s
τd = 70.72/s N/mm²

Step 3 — Eccentric moment

M = Pe
M = 25,000(500) = 12,500,000 N·mm

Step 4 — Weld-group second moment of area

For the stated rectangular group, take bending about the vertical centroidal axis. The top and bottom welds each have length 150 mm and the two side welds each have length 100 mm.

Iy,line = 2150³12 + 2(100)(75²)
2(150³/12) = 562,500 mm³
2(100)(75²) = 1,125,000 mm³
Iy,line = 1,687,500 mm³

Multiplying by the effective throat gives the actual weld-group second moment:

Iw = 0.707s(1,687,500) = 1,193,062.5s mm⁴

Step 5 — Bending stress at the extreme weld fibre

σb = MyIw
y = 75 mm
σb = [12,500,000(75)]/[1,193,062.5s]
σb = 785.8/s N/mm²

Step 6 — Combined weld stress

τmax = 12√(σb² + 4τd²)
75 = 0.5√[(785.8/s)² + 4(70.72/s)²]
150 = √[(785.8/s)² + 4(70.72/s)²]
22,500s² = 617,481.6 + 20,006.1
22,500s² = 637,487.7
s² = 28.333
s = 5.32 mm

Step 7 — Adopt and re-check

Adopt s = 6 mm.
τd = 70.72/6 = 11.79 N/mm²
σb = 785.8/6 = 130.97 N/mm²
τmax = 0.5√(130.97² + 4(11.79²))
τmax ≈ 66.56 N/mm²

66.56 < 75 N/mm² → Safe.

Final answer: Adopt 6 mm fillet weld.

2. Cotter Joint — Practice

Generated practice: Exam-style cotter-joint calculation; not an official PYQ.

Given: Rod diameter d = 50 mm; Syt = 380 N/mm²; FOS = 6. Use Syc = 2Syt and Ssy = 0.5Syt for this practice problem.

To find: Axial design load and the starting load basis for the joint dimensions.

Step 1 — Allowable tensile stress

σt,allow = SytFOS
σt,allow = 380/6 = 63.33 N/mm²

Step 2 — Allowable compressive stress

σc,allow = SycFOS = 2SytFOS
σc,allow = 760/6 = 126.67 N/mm²

Step 3 — Allowable shear stress

τallow = SsyFOS = 0.5(380)6
τallow = 31.67 N/mm²

Step 4 — Axial load based on the rod section

P = πd²4σt,allow
P = (π/4)(50²)(63.33)
50² = 2,500 mm²
π(2,500)/4 = 1,963.50 mm²
P = 1,963.50(63.33)
P ≈ 124,348 N = 124.35 kN

Step 5 — Joint checks to continue

Use P = 124.35 kN as the design load and check the rod, spigot, socket and cotter against the failure modes required by the design method: tension, double shear, bearing/crushing and tearing. Standard proportions must be taken from the permitted PSG Design Data Book or the exact prescribed design procedure.

Exam warning: The rod load alone does not complete a cotter-joint design. The weakest connected part governs the final dimensions.

3. Eccentrically Loaded Bolted Joint — Fully Worked Practice

Generated practice: This is a numerical exercise for the eccentric-bolt-group method, not an official PYQ.

Given: Four identical bolts are arranged symmetrically at the corners of a 100 mm × 100 mm square. A load P = 8 kN acts at an eccentricity e = 200 mm. Assume the moment-induced load is distributed by the standard elastic bolt-group method.

To find: Maximum bolt load.

Step 1 — Direct load per bolt

Fd = Pn
Fd = 8/4 = 2.00 kN per bolt

Step 2 — Eccentric moment

M = Pe
M = 8(200) = 1600 kN·mm

Step 3 — Bolt distance from centroid

Each bolt is 50 mm from the centroid in both directions.

r = √(50² + 50²) = √5000 = 70.71 mm

Step 4 — Sum of squared bolt distances

Σr² = 4(70.71²) = 4(5000) = 20,000 mm²

Step 5 — Moment-induced load at each bolt

Fmi = MriΣr²
Fmi = (1600 × 70.71)/20,000
Fmi = 5.657 kN

Step 6 — Resultant critical-bolt load

The direct load and moment-induced load must be vectorially combined using the bolt's actual load direction. For this symmetric square arrangement, the critical bolt is one of the bolts on the load-leading side. A conservative scalar addition for exam practice is:

Fmax,conservative = Fd + Fmi
Fmax,conservative = 2.00 + 5.657 = 7.657 kN
Method note: The 7.657 kN value is a conservative scalar practice result. In a full exam solution, draw the bolt-group load vectors and perform the prescribed vector resolution before final bolt sizing.

4. Weld Group under Eccentric Load — 12-Mark Answer Workflow

Generated workflow: Use actual dimensions/material data from the question; this section is not a PYQ.
  1. Draw the weld group and show P and eccentricity e.
  2. Convert fillet size s into effective throat 0.707s.
  3. Calculate weld-group effective area.
  4. Locate the centroid.
  5. Calculate the relevant line second moment/polar moment for the weld geometry.
  6. Calculate direct shear or direct normal stress.
  7. Calculate M = Pe.
  8. Find moment-induced stress at the critical location.
  9. Combine stresses using the criterion required by the prescribed method.
  10. Solve for theoretical weld size.
  11. Adopt a practical standard size.
  12. Recalculate actual stress and state safe/unsafe.
