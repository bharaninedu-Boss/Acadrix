1. Overhang Crank – Q11(a)

Given:

P = 1 kN = 1000 N; Syt = 400 N/mm²; FOS = 2; vertical distance = 500 mm; eccentricity = 50 + 100 + 25 = 175 mm. Maximum shear stress theory.

To find:

Diameter at section XX.

Solution:

Allowable shear stressτallow=Syt2×FOS

τallow = 400/(2 × 2) = 100 N/mm².

Bending momentM=P×L

M = 1000 × 500 = 500 × 10³ Nmm.

TorqueT=P×e

T = 1000 × 175 = 175 × 10³ Nmm.

Solid circular sectionσb=32Mπd3;τ=16Tπd3
Maximum shear stress theoryτmax=σb22+τ2

Substitution gives d ≈ 29.8 mm.

Check: τmax ≤ τallow.

Result: d ≈ 30 mm.

2. Fatigue Plate – Q11(b)

Given:

Completely reversed axial load = ±50 kN; Sut = 630 N/mm²; reliability = 90%; FOS = 2; size factor = 0.85; notch radius = 5 mm; b = 50 mm.

To find:

Plate thickness t for infinite fatigue life.

Solution:

Unmodified endurance limitS′e=Sut2

S′e = 630/2 = 315 N/mm².

Fatigue factors: Ka = 0.76, Kb = 0.85, Kc = 0.897, Kd = 0.496.

Corrected endurance limitSe=Ka×Kb×Kc×Kd×S′e

Se = 0.76 × 0.85 × 0.897 × 0.496 × 315 = 90.54 N/mm².

For the stated notch condition, Kf = 2.016. The design condition used here gives allowable alternating stress = 0.8Se/FOS.

Alternating stressσa=0.8SeFOS

σa = (0.8 × 90.54)/2 = 36.216 N/mm².

Axial stressσa=Pbt

36.216 = 50000/(50t).

t = 50000/(50 × 36.216) = 27.61 mm.

Check: adopted t ≥ calculated t.

Result: t = 27.6 mm; adopt t = 28 mm.

3. Square Key – Q12(a)

Given:

d = 25 mm; P = 15 kW = 15000 W; N = 720 rpm; 50C4 key; Syt = 460 N/mm²; FOS = 3.

To find:

Suitable square-key dimensions.

Solution:

Power–torque relationT=60P2πN

T = [60 × 15000/(2π × 720)] × 10³ = 198944 Nmm.

Allowable shear stressτallow=Syt2×FOS

τallow = 460/(2 × 3) = 76.67 N/mm².

Allowable crushing stress = 460/3 = 153.33 N/mm².

For a square key, theoretical b = t ≈ d/4 = 6.25 mm.

Key shear strengthT=τallowbld2

Using b = 6.25 mm gives l ≈ 33.2 mm. Crushing is checked before final selection.

Check: key shear and crushing stresses are within allowable values.

Result: Adopt 7 × 7 × 35 mm square key.

4. Muff Coupling – Q12(b)

Given:

P = 25 kW; N = 360 rpm; shaft Syt = 400 N/mm²; sleeve Sut = 200 N/mm²; FOS = 4 for shaft/key and 6 for sleeve.

To find:

Shaft diameter, sleeve dimensions and key dimensions.

Solution:

TorqueT=60P2πN

T = [60 × 25000/(2π × 360)] × 10³ = 663146 Nmm.

Allowable shaft shear stressτallow=Syt2×FOS

τallow = 400/(2 × 4) = 50 N/mm².

Solid shaft torsionT=π16τallowd3

Calculated d = 40.73 mm → select d = 45 mm.

Using the applicable sleeve proportion: D ≈ 2d + 13 = 103 mm → 105 mm; L ≈ 3.5d = 157.5 mm → 160 mm.

Sleeve allowable shear = 200/(2 × 6) = 16.67 N/mm²; calculated sleeve stress ≈ 3.02 N/mm².

For a 45 mm shaft, applicable PSG key = 14 × 9 mm; required length ≈ 80 mm.

Check shaft, sleeve and key separately.

Result: d = 45 mm; D = 105 mm; L = 160 mm; key = 14 × 9 × 80 mm.

5. Double-Threaded Trapezoidal Power Screw – Q13(a)

Given:

W = 300 kN = 300000 N; d = 100 mm; pitch p = 12 mm; double thread; μ = 0.15; collar friction neglected.

To find:

Raising torque, lowering torque and efficiency.

Solution:

For the stated ISO metric trapezoidal thread, d₂ = 94 mm.

LeadLead=2p

Lead = 2 × 12 = 24 mm.

Equivalent friction coefficientμ′=μ sec β

β = 15°, μ′ = 0.15 sec15° = 0.15529.

Helix angletan α=Leadπd2

α = tan⁻¹[24/(π × 94)] = 4.646°.

Friction angleφ=tan−1μ′

φ = tan⁻¹(0.15529) = 8.827°.

Raising torqueTR=Wd22 tan(φ+α)

TR ≈ 3378 N·m.

Lowering torqueTL=Wd22 tan(φ−α)

TL ≈ 1031 N·m.

Efficiencyη=W×Lead2πTR×100

η ≈ 33.9%.

Result: TR ≈ 3378 N·m; TL ≈ 1031 N·m; η ≈ 33.9%.

6. Rectangular Fillet Weld – Q13(b)

Given:

P = 25 kN = 25000 N; e = 500 mm; weld rectangle = 150 × 100 mm; permissible shear = 75 N/mm².

To find:

Fillet weld size s.

Solution:

Fillet-weld throatt=0.707s

Total weld length = 2(150 + 100) = 500 mm.

Direct weld shearτd=P0.707sL

τd = 25000/(0.707 × s × 500) = 70.72/s N/mm².

Eccentric momentM=Pe

M = 25000 × 500 = 12.5 × 10⁶ Nmm.

For the rectangular weld group, Z ≈ 15907.5s mm³.

Moment-induced stressτm=MZ

τm ≈ 785.8/s N/mm².

Combined maximum shearτmax=12σb2+4τd2

Putting τmax = 75 N/mm² gives s ≈ 5.32 mm.

Check: selected weld size ≥ calculated size.

Result: s ≈ 5.32 mm; adopt 6 mm fillet weld.

7. Hydrodynamic Journal Bearing – Q14(b)

Given:

W = 3.2 kN; N = 1490 rpm; d = 50 mm; L = 50 mm; c = 0.05 mm; viscosity = 25 cP.

To find:

f, power loss, minimum film thickness, oil flow and temperature rise.

Solution:

Bearing pressurep=WLd

p = 3200/(50 × 50) = 1.28 N/mm².

For L/d = 1, Sommerfeld number ≈ 0.121. From the applicable chart, f ≈ 0.00644 and hmin/c ≈ 0.4.

Minimum film thicknesshmin=0.4c

hmin = 0.4 × 0.05 = 0.020 mm.

Surface speedv=πdN60

v = π × 0.05 × 1490/60 = 3.901 m/s.

Friction power lossP=fWv

P ≈ 80.4 W.

Chart oil flow ≈ 0.403 L/min. Under the stated heat-carrying assumption, temperature rise ≈ 7.9°C.

Result: f ≈ 0.00644; power loss ≈ 80.4 W; hmin ≈ 0.020 mm; oil flow ≈ 0.403 L/min; temperature rise ≈ 7.9°C.

8. Line Shaft with Two Pulleys – Q16(a)

Given:

Tight tension = 2.7 kN; tension ratio = 3:1; shaft 40C8; Sut = 650 N/mm²; Syt = 380 N/mm²; keyed pulleys; Kb = 1.5; Kt = 1.0; pulley A = 250 mm; pulley B = 450 mm.

To find:

Critical bending moment and shaft diameter.

Solution:

At A: T₁ = 2700 N and T₂ = 900 N.

Torque from belt tensionsT=(T1−T2)D2

T = (2700 − 900)(250/2) = 225000 Nmm.

At B: T₃ = 1500 N and T₄ = 500 N; horizontal belt pull = 2000 N.

Static equilibrium gives vertical reactions RC ≈ 2191 N, RD ≈ 1409 N; horizontal reactions RC ≈ 435 N, RD ≈ 1565 N.

At A: Mv ≈ 986087 Nmm and Mh ≈ 195652 Nmm.

Resultant bending momentMb=Mv2+Mh2

Mb ≈ 1.005 × 10⁶ Nmm.

ASME allowable shear stressτallow=min(0.30Syt,0.18Sut)

τallow = min(114,117) = 114 N/mm². Keyway reduction gives 85.5 N/mm².

ASME combined bending and torsiond3=16πτallowKbMb2+KtT2

Substitution gives d ≈ 44.95 mm.

Check selected standard diameter against allowable stress.

Result: Adopt d = 45 mm.

9. Socket/Cotter Joint – Q16(b)

Given:

Rod diameter = 50 mm; cotter plate thickness = 15 mm; 40C8; Syt = 380 N/mm²; compressive yield = 2 × tensile yield; shear yield = 50% tensile yield; FOS = 6.

To find:

Socket dimensions.

Solution:

Allowable tensile stressσt,allow=SytFOS

σt,allow = 380/6 = 63.33 N/mm².

Allowable compressive stressσc,allow=2SytFOS

σc,allow = 760/6 = 126.67 N/mm².

Allowable shear stressτallow=0.5SytFOS

τallow = (0.5 × 380)/6 = 31.67 N/mm².

Rod loadP=π4d2σt,allow

P = (π/4)(50²)(63.33) = 124.35 kN.

Apply the socket-end tensile, compressive and shear checks. Theoretical dimensions are approximately: inside diameter 60.45 mm, outside diameter 84.21 mm, collar diameter 130.44 mm, a = 28.04 mm, c = 30.20 mm.

Check and round upward to practical standard values.

Result: inside diameter = 65 mm; outside diameter = 85 mm; collar diameter = 135 mm; a = 30 mm; c = 35 mm.
