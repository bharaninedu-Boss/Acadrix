Fundamental Concepts in Design • ACADRIX • R-2021

Part A — 2-Mark Questions

1. What is machine design?
Answer: It is the systematic process of developing machine elements or systems to perform a required function safely, reliably and economically.
2. What is factor of safety?
Answer: FOS = Failure stress / Allowable stress.
3. Why are preferred numbers used?
Answer: They standardize sizes and reduce unnecessary varieties of dimensions and products.
4. Define stress concentration.
Answer: It is the localized increase in stress caused by geometric discontinuities such as holes, keyways, grooves or sharp corners.
5. What is eccentric loading?
Answer: A load whose line of action does not pass through the centroidal axis of the section, producing direct stress together with a moment.
6. Why does a curved beam have a different stress distribution from a straight beam?
Answer: The neutral axis shifts from the centroidal axis because the inner and outer fibers have different lengths.
7. What is endurance limit?
Answer: It is the stress amplitude associated with the fatigue strength of a material for the specified long-life condition.
8. Distinguish strength and stiffness.
Answer: Strength concerns resistance to yielding or fracture; stiffness concerns resistance to excessive elastic deformation.

Self-Check Answers

  • Q1: Preferred numbers standardize product dimensions; FOS compares failure and allowable stress.
  • Q2: Eccentric load = direct stress + bending effect.
  • Q3: Use σ = P/A ± M/Z for the relevant section; identify the largest tensile/compressive value.
  • Q4: For a rectangular curved section, rn = h / ln(ro/ri); rn is below rc for the usual curved-beam geometry.
  • Q5: Rankine uses principal normal stress; Tresca uses maximum shear stress; von Mises uses distortion energy.
  • Q6: Modifiers account for real-service effects that reduce fatigue strength from the laboratory specimen value.
