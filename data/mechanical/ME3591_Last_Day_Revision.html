2. Unit-Wise Must Remember

Unit I — Fundamental Concepts in Design

  • Design process; factors influencing machine design; material selection; preferred numbers.
  • Direct, bending and torsional loading; modes of failure; FOS.
  • Combined loads, principal stresses and eccentric loading.
  • Curved beams, crane hook and C-frame.
  • Theories of failure; strength versus stiffness; stress concentration.
  • Fluctuating stresses, endurance limit, finite/infinite life and standards.

Unit II — Design of Shafts and Couplings

  • Design solid/hollow shafts based on strength, rigidity and critical speed.
  • Separate bending moment and transmitted torque.
  • For keys, check shear and crushing.
  • Use PSG Design Data Book for standard dimensions where applicable.

3. Numerical Attack Method

  1. Given — write every value with units.
  2. Find — list exactly what is required.
  3. Sketch/FBD — draw one when geometry or reactions matter.
  4. Allowable stress — apply the given FOS and failure theory.
  5. Formula — write the governing equation before substitution.
  6. Calculate — keep N–mm–MPa or another consistent unit system.
  7. Check — verify stress, dimensions and physical reasonableness.
  8. Standardize — use the applicable PSG standard dimension.
  9. Box final answer with units.

4. PYQ Practice Priorities

  • Overhang crank — combined bending and torsion.
  • Fatigue plate — completely reversed loading and endurance limit.
  • Square-key design.
  • Muff-coupling design.
  • Double-threaded trapezoidal power screw — raising/lowering torque and efficiency.
  • Rectangular fillet-welded joint under eccentric load.
  • Hydrodynamic journal-bearing numerical.
  • Line-shaft design with pulleys.
  • Cotter/socket-joint dimensions.
